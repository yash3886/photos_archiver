Privacy Policy

Photos Archiver is a personal tool used only by its developer, with their own Google account.

Data accessed

Google Drive: reads the owner's Google Takeout export files and creates organized copies of the photos and videos, plus processing reports, in the owner's own Drive. Gmail: sends status emails to the owner only (send-only; no emails are read).

Use, storage and sharing

Data is processed in the owner's own Google Cloud project and written back only to the owner's own Google Drive. No data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party, and nothing is used for advertising or model training. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
